🚀 Step-by-Step Guide for Beginners

Follow these simple visual steps to understand how the platform works from registration to admin management.

Step 1: Attendee Registration & Ticket Generation

The journey begins at the Home Portal where your event attendees sign up.

  • Sign Up: Attendees click "Register" and fill out a quick, mobile-friendly form with their Full Name, Email, and Phone Number. They must explicitly agree to the Data Privacy Policy and Marketing Data Gathering via dual checkboxes.
  • Social Media Gate: To boost your event's online presence, attendees must complete mandatory "Social Missions" (like following your Facebook page or joining a Telegram group).
  • Verification Countdown: When they click a social link, a smart 10-second countdown runs before they are verified.
  • QR Pass Generation: Once verified, the system generates a unique, secure QR Code ticket (e.g., BQF-A72J9) that they can screenshot and show at the event!

Step 2: Admin Dashboard & Creating Quests

Event organizers and staff control everything behind the scenes in the Admin Dashboard.

  • Secure Login: Admins log in via a hidden portal using their secure credentials. The system automatically recognizes their role (Superadmin, Verifier, Viewer, etc.).
  • 12 Quick Preset Templates: In the "⚡ Event Quests" tab, click "+ Add Quest" to open the builder. Use 1-click presets (Social Follow, Join Telegram, Event Feedback Note, Booth Passcode, Booth Photo + Review, Networking Buddy, Keynote Takeaway, Trivia Quiz, Stage Selfie, Swag Bag Claim, Workshop Code, Instant Check-in) to pre-fill quests in seconds!
  • 6 Verification Modes: Choose between ⚡ Instant Claim, 📷 Screenshot Proof, 💬 Messagebox Note, 📸+💬 Photo + Message, ❓ Trivia Quiz, or 🔑 Secret Passcode.
  • Admin Reviewer Attribution: Every approved or rejected submission records the reviewing admin's email (e.g. 👮 admin@blockquest.ph) and timestamp, visible in both review tables and the master CSV audit log!
  • Advanced Rules: Configure time-limited ⏱️ Flash Quests (countdown timers) or 🔒 Prerequisite Chains (locked quests).
  • Live Mobile Simulation: As you type, the right-hand panel renders an exact real-time preview of the mobile quest card.
  • Publishing: Save it as a "Draft" to hide it temporarily, or set it to "Live" to immediately broadcast it to attendee phones!

Step 3: Scanning Tickets at the Entrance

The platform turns any smartphone or tablet into a lightning-fast entrance ticket scanner.

  • Open Scanner: Entrance staff members tap the "📷 QR Scanner" tab on the Admin Dashboard to activate their device's camera.
  • Instant Scan: The attendee holds out their phone with their downloaded QR ticket. The camera reads it in milliseconds.
  • Validation: If valid, a large green "Valid Ticket" badge appears on the screen, granting access.
  • Auto Check-in: The attendee is automatically marked as "Checked-in" in the database, updating the live attendance statistics.

Step 4: Vendor Booth Scanner (Awarding XP)

Vendor booths use a specialized scanner mode to reward attendees for visiting their stations.

  • Booth Login: Vendor staff access the /booth-scan URL on their phones and log in using their unique booth email and password.
  • Ready to Scan: Their phone camera activates instantly, ready to scan the attendees' event QR passes.
  • XP Reward: When an attendee visits the booth and presents their QR code, the vendor scans it.
  • Instant Points: The attendee is immediately awarded a preset amount of XP (e.g., +150 XP) on their profile, encouraging them to visit more booths!
  • Data Tracking: The system records exactly which booth scanned which attendee, giving organizers rich data on booth traffic.

⚡ Quest Engine: Capabilities, Modes & Real-World Examples

The BlockQuest Quest System is a gamified engagement engine designed to drive physical venue traffic, boost social media viral reach, test attendee knowledge, and reward active participation.

🎯 The 6 Quest Verification Modes

⚡

1. Instant Claim

How it Works: Attendees click the claim button and immediately receive XP points without requiring proof or review.

Best For: Daily logins, reading event rules, clicking resource links, or welcoming onboarding tasks.

Example: "Daily Community Check-in" (+50 XP).

📷

2. Screenshot Proof

How it Works: Attendees upload a photo or screenshot. The submission routes strictly to the Quest Verifications queue for 1-click review.

Best For: Social media follows, reposts, selfies at the keynote stage, or community group invites.

Example: "Follow @BlockQuest on X & Submit Screenshot" (+100 XP).

💬

3. Messagebox Note

How it Works: Attendees submit written feedback, thoughts, or answers in a dedicated text box. Routes strictly to the Message Notes queue.

Best For: Event surveys, speaker questions, product feedback, or sharing takeaways.

Example: "Share Your Event Feedback" (+150 XP).

📸+💬

4. Photo + Message

How it Works: Combines visual image proof with an accompanying written text note. Shows full photo thumbnail and message banner in Verifications queue.

Best For: Booth review photos, speaker selfies with key learnings, or networking photos with quester handles.

Example: "Booth Photo & Feedback" (+250 XP).

❓

5. Trivia Quiz Question

How it Works: Organizers configure a secret answer string. Attendees must type the correct answer (case-insensitive) on their app to unlock XP instantly.

Best For: Speaker Q&A sessions, sponsor trivia challenges, or easter eggs hidden in presentation slides.

Example: "What year was the Bitcoin whitepaper published?" (Answer: 2008) (+150 XP).

🔑

6. Secret Passcode

How it Works: Organizers assign a 4-to-8 character secret code (e.g. BOOTH-01). Booth staff or keynote speakers verbally reveal the code to attendees with instant retry error guidance.

Best For: Driving physical foot traffic to sponsor booths, workshop rooms, and swag claim counters.

Example: "Visit Sponsor Booth in Zone 2" (Passcode: BOOTH-01) (+150 XP).

Database Architecture

Why Admin Quest Deletions Never Corrupt XP

The architectural principle separating the running wallet from historical receipts.

💰 registrations.total_xp

The Running User Wallet

Stored directly on the user record for ultra-fast leaderboard and profile indexing during high-traffic live events. Never requires expensive runtime SUM calculations.

The Immutable Audit Receipt

Stores exact XP awarded at the moment of approval. If an admin edits a quest XP value or deletes the quest, the user keeps their earned balance safely intact.

ENTERPRISE GRADE PROTECTION

🔒 System Security & Data Privacy Architecture

Comprehensive safeguards defending attendee data, credentials, administrative routes, and financial point economies.

1

scryptSync Password Hashing

• Salted Cryptography: Passwords are never stored in plaintext. Hashed using Node.js's native node:crypto scryptSync with a unique 16-byte random salt and 64-byte key.
• Hardware Resistant: Memory-hard algorithm that defends against GPU/ASIC brute-force and rainbow table attacks.

2

Strict Unique Constraints

• Duplicate Prevention: PostgreSQL UNIQUE(email) and UNIQUE(ticket_code) constraints stop account spoofing and double-claiming at the database engine level.
• Normalized Email Inputs: All emails are trimmed and converted to lowercase on entry to eliminate casing bypasses.

3

Role-Based Access Control

• Enforced API Authorization: Admin endpoints verify role tiers (superadmin, admin, manage_attendees, verifier, viewer).
• Least-Privilege Isolation: Verifiers only access image review queues; gate staff only access ticket scanners.

4

Service Role Key Isolation

• Zero Browser Leakage: Sensitive SUPABASE_SERVICE_ROLE_KEY is strictly bound to server-side Node.js runtimes.

KNOWLEDGE BASE & OPERATIONS

❓ Master Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Comprehensive operational answers categorized by Gate Operations, Attendee Issues, Quest Management, and System Security.

1. What happens if an attendee's phone dies or their screen is too cracked to scan? Gate Operations
Gate staff can switch to the 🎫 Attendees tab on any device, type the attendee's name, email, or ticket code (BQF-XXXXXX) into the search bar, and click "Check In". The safety confirmation modal will appear to confirm their identity before marking them as checked-in.
2. How does the system prevent accidental duplicate check-ins at the gate? Gate Operations
The camera scanner has a built-in safety confirmation modal that forces the operator to review the attendee's profile before confirming. If the pass was already scanned at another gate, the system instantly throws a red "Already Checked In" banner with the exact timestamp.
3. Can Superadmins add or remove social follow missions during the live event? Superadmin
Yes! All social missions are stored dynamically in the Supabase database. Superadmins can click "+ Add Mission" to add Discord channels, sponsor pages, or partner links at any time. Changes reflect immediately for all subsequent registrants without needing application rebuilds.
4. If an admin deletes or edits a Quest's XP value, will users lose their earned points? XP Economy
No, user points are 100% safe. The platform uses a dual-table model:
  • registrations.total_xp holds the running wallet balance.
  • quest_completions.xp_awarded stores an immutable receipt of what was granted at the time of completion.
Deleting a quest removes it from the catalog, but previously claimed XP remains securely deposited in the attendee's wallet.
5. What happens when a quest proof submission is rejected by a Verifier? Verifications
When rejecting a proof, the Verifier is required to submit a reason (e.g. "Blurry photo", "Wrong booth selfie"). The submission status updates to "Rejected" with the feedback displayed to the user, allowing them to re-take the photo and submit again.
6. How secure are attendee and staff passwords? Security
Passwords are never stored in plain text. Both attendee passwords and admin staff credentials are cryptographically salted and hashed using Node.js's native crypto.scryptSync algorithm (16-byte random salt and 64-byte key length).
7. Does the attendee need active cellular data to present their QR Pass at the venue? Attendee Guide
No. Once generated, attendees can screenshot their QR pass or save the image to their gallery. The gate camera scanner decodes the encrypted ticket code directly from the screenshot without requiring the attendee to have an active data connection.
8. How does the system handle simultaneous check-ins across 5 different gate entrances? Infrastructure
All scan actions perform atomic updates directly against the central Supabase PostgreSQL database. If Gate 1 checks in Ticket BQF-X79K2M, Gate 2 will see it as checked-in within milliseconds, preventing ticket-sharing attacks.
9. Where are quest proof images stored, and is there a storage quota? Infrastructure
Proof screenshots are streamed directly to a dedicated Supabase S3 bucket (blockquestbucket). The client pre-compresses images before streaming, ensuring fast uploads and virtually unlimited scalable storage.
10. Can a Superadmin immediately terminate a staff member's access if a device is lost? Superadmin
Yes. The Superadmin can open the 🛡️ Staff / Admins tab and click the red "Delete" button next to the account. Because permissions are checked on every API request, access is revoked instantly across all active sessions.
11. How do Booths and Vendors award fixed scores on an attendee's first visit? Booth Operations
Vendors open the dedicated 🏪 Booth Scanner (/booth-scan) and authenticate with their station credentials (accessible to superadmin, admin, and booth_staff). When attendees present their QR pass, the vendor camera scans it and awards a 1-time fixed XP score (e.g. +150 XP) configured in advance by the Superadmin. The XP score is securely locked on the scanner UI to prevent vendor tampering. Subsequent scans on the same booth return an "Already Visited" status to prevent double-claiming. For maximum security, closing the browser or tab automatically terminates the station session.
12. How do engineers stress test registration, gate check-in, booth scan, and admin endpoints? Performance & Load
The system features a dedicated interactive load testing portal at /stress-test and a terminal CLI script (npm run stress-test). This allows developers to simulate concurrent bursts of 50–500 requests across registration, gate check-in, booth visits, and admin dashboard polling to verify latency, throughput (TPS), and database connection pool health before live event day.
13. How does the Attendee Security PIN Code protection & Admin Reset work? Account Security
When an attendee logs in for the first time without a PIN, the app prompts them to create a 4-to-6 digit Security PIN code. On future logins, the login form detects the PIN requirement and displays the PIN input field box.

Forgot PIN Recovery: If an attendee forgets their PIN, they can approach an Event Staff or Admin at the helpdesk. The admin opens the 🎫 Attendees tab, searches for the quester, and clicks "🔑 Reset PIN" to auto-generate a 4-digit temporary PIN code for the attendee.
14. How do CSV exports and category filters work across Admin Dashboard tables? Data Reporting
All major admin tables (Attendees, Quest Log, Quest Verifications, Message Notes) include a green 📥 Export CSV button. The export dynamically reads your active filters (Search keywords, Status, Category, or Verification Mode) so you only export the exact slice of data you need.
15. How can Superadmins hide social missions without deleting them? Social Missions
Superadmins can click the 🙈 Hide / 👁️ Show toggle button in the Social Missions tab. Hiding a mission updates its is_active status to false, which immediately removes it from public attendee registration forms while keeping historical records intact.
